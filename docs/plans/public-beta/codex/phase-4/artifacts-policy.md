Lane "artifacts-policy": the pixel capture policy, artifact paths, retention and safe reads. Phase 4 item 5 of docs/plans/public-beta/release-0.1.0.md. Read docs/plans/public-beta/codex/phase-4/common.md first; it binds this lane.

What exists: the run folder and its store (src/store/run-store.ts, read-run-folder.ts), screenshot references in the events (src/protocol/evidence.ts), text redaction of secrets everywhere text is written (src/runner/secrets.ts and the redactors the drivers use), the diagnostics artifacts (src/diagnostics/artifact.ts, sanitize.ts). Nothing decides yet what may be captured as pixels, where a media artifact may live, how long it is kept, or how a report reads it safely.

Deliver two small modules with complete tests, and their documentation:
1. Artifact paths and safe reads (src/store/artifacts.ts): one function that gives the path of an artifact from its record identity and kind (screenshot, thumbnail, recording, frame sequence, diagnostics, report), always inside the run folder, with names that carry no app text and no secret; portable references (relative to the run folder) and their resolution; a read that refuses anything outside the run folder (`..`, absolute paths, symlinks and hard links that leave it, a folder swapped for a link between the check and the open), anything over a stated size, and anything that is not a regular file, with a named reason; a listing of what a run folder holds against what its events reference, naming both the missing and the unreferenced.
2. Retention (same module): what is kept and what is removed when (partial files of lost recordings, recordings of passed tests when the configuration says to keep only failures, thumbnails), applied only at stated moments and only inside the run folder, recorded in an event shape you propose to the orchestrator; nothing is ever deleted that the events still reference as present.
3. The pixel capture policy (src/media/policy.ts): a documented, typed policy saying, for each app of a test, whether screenshots and recordings may be taken, and what happens around a secret: text redaction does not redact pixels, so while a secret is being typed into a field that shows its text, capture for that app is withheld from just before the first key until the field no longer shows it or the page leaves, and the withheld stretch is recorded as withheld by policy, never as dropped and never as "nothing happened"; a field the app itself masks (a password field) may stay captured, and the policy says how that is decided from what the driver can observe, erring towards withholding when it cannot tell. The policy also covers screenshots taken for AI checks and at failure. Write the policy as a section of the guide in plain words, with its limits stated (what it cannot protect: a secret the app later displays on its own).
4. HTML safety helper: one escaping function for untrusted app text placed into HTML text and attribute positions, and one for URLs, with tests built from hostile strings; the HTML report lane uses these and nothing of its own.

As soon as the exported signatures are stable, send the orchestrator a short message (SendMessage to "main") with them: the recording-runner and HTML report lanes build on them.

Files you own: src/store/artifacts.ts (new), src/media/policy.ts (new), src/reporters/html/escape.ts (new), their unit tests, the guide's sections on the capture policy and on artifacts, docs/plans/public-beta/proofs/artifacts-policy.md. Do not edit any other file; the recording-runner lane wires your modules into the runner and the configuration.

Gates before you report: your unit files, `npm run test:unit`, `npm run test:types`, and under the lock `npm run typecheck`. The safe read is shown against real symlink, hard link and swap attempts in a temporary folder.
